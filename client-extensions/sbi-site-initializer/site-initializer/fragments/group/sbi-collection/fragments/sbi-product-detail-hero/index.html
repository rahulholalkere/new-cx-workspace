Required Documents Checklist

Keep these digital or physical copies ready for branch verification pickup:

Identity & Address Proof

  • PAN Card (Mandatory for tax identification)
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID card
  • Recent passport-size photographs of all applicants

Income Proof (Salaried)

  • Salary Slips for the last 3 months
  • Bank account statements for the last 6 months
  • Copy of Form 16 or Income Tax Returns for last 2 years

Property Documents

  • Registered Sale Agreement / Allotment Letter
  • Approved Building Plan & Commencement Certificate
  • NOC from Builder / Housing Society
